# History taken out of AGENTS.md (2026-11-08)

The maintainer's project-structure standard keeps history out of rule text: a rule file holds operative rules, and
the dates, incidents and provenance beside them live in `docs/decisions/`. When AGENTS.md was brought to that form,
the dated passages below moved here, under the rule they explain; the rules themselves stayed in AGENTS.md in their
own words, and its sections now run visibility, purpose, scope boundary, layout, tasks, commands, verification, then
the rules for working here. No rule was removed as a restatement of a rule held elsewhere: every rule in AGENTS.md
binds this repo's own committed content or the estate it installs, and the work-side sessions that read it read no
other rule home.

## Visibility

- The original, stronger claim behind the credential-free channel was that no personal credential ever touches the
  corp machine. It was superseded on 2026-08-15 by the graded-boundary ruling (design principle 17): the user's own
  credentials may land in revocable per-device, footprint-registered form (see `corpus/boundary-protocol.md`,
  credential rule).
- Committed content was held to public-grade discipline from birth. The repo was founded on 2026-08-03, entirely
  before the maintainer's employment start date, which is the basis of the clean-provenance statement.
